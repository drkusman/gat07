About events

  • Published events with a date today or later show as "upcoming" on the public home page.
  • Leave photos and video blank until they're ready — the public page shows a "coming soon" note automatically.
  • Draft (unpublished) events are only visible here in the coordination centre.
  • An event with uploaded photos gets its own tab in the Photo Gallery slideshow on the public Events page.
  • Uploading a video clip here gives the public event card a "Watch video" link that plays it directly.
  • An event's scope is fixed at creation from who created it: State Coordinators create state-only events, Zonal Coordinators create zone-wide events, and Admin-created events are nationwide. It can't be changed after saving.
  • Events submitted by a Media Coordinator or National Publicity Secretary are held for Admin approval before they appear on the public site; editing one after approval sends it back for re-approval.
