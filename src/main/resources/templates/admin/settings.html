How the age limit works

  • Age is calculated from the date of birth entered at registration, in whole years, on the day of registration.
  • Anyone younger than the minimum age cannot complete registration and sees a clear message explaining why.
  • Leave the maximum age blank for no upper limit.
  • When date of birth is required, members must supply it; otherwise the limits only apply to members who do.
  • Existing members are not removed when you change the limits; the rules apply the next time they edit their profile.
  • Nigeria's voting age is 18. A lower minimum lets youth mobilisers register ahead of voter registration.
