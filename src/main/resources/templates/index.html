How it works

  1. Register with your phone number and choose your polling unit down to its exact map location.
  2. Share your referral link with friends, family and neighbours in your ward.
  3. Report activities, incidents and voter mobilisation progress from the field.
  4. Coordinators monitor coverage across all 6 zones, 37 states, 774 LGAs and every ward.
